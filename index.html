AutoMix Support

A simple guide to setting up AutoMix and enjoying smoother transitions between songs.

Before you start

What you need

Make sure AutoMix is set up in the app and that you are listening on this iPhone. Start playback from a playlist or album so another track is waiting in the queue.

Important: Turn off Spotify's built-in Crossfade. AutoMix should not be used at the same time as Karaoke or Lossless audio.

Turn on AutoMix

Enable the feature

  1. Open the app and go to Mod Settings.
  2. Choose AutoMix.
  3. Switch AutoMix on.
  4. Start a playlist or album and let a song play.

AutoMix starts preparing a transition after you have listened to a song for at least 10 seconds. Keep listening and let the current song reach its ending for the transition to play.

Choose your transition

AutoMix · 32 beats

Choose AutoMix · 32 beats for the standard AutoMix transition. This is the default option and is designed to blend the end of one track into the beginning of the next.

Spotify transition

Choose Spotify transition to customise the transition length. Select 2, 4 or 8 bars. You can also enable Vocals first to bring the incoming song's vocals in before its instruments.

Adjust when preparation starts

Use Start preparing to choose how far ahead of the end of a song AutoMix should begin getting the transition ready. You can leave the default value if you are unsure.

Check the status

Know when AutoMix is ready

In Mod Settings → AutoMix, check the Now status to see what AutoMix is doing. Last transition shows the result of the most recent attempt.

Try it now

Want to check AutoMix without waiting for the whole song? Open the AutoMix settings and tap Try it now. This moves playback close to the point where preparation begins. The transition will be heard near the end of the song.

Tips for the best experience

Keep playback uninterrupted

For the best chance of a transition, keep the playlist or album playing and let the song continue. Skipping to another track or seeking to a different position cancels the pending transition, and normal playback takes over.

Troubleshooting

AutoMix does not start preparing

Check that AutoMix is enabled, that you have listened to the current song for at least 10 seconds, and that another track is queued. Make sure the song has enough time remaining for the selected transition.

The transition does not play

Keep Spotify's Crossfade turned off. Make sure Karaoke and Lossless are not active, and avoid skipping or seeking while AutoMix is preparing the transition.

The next song is not picked up

Start playback from a playlist or album and check that a next track is shown in the queue. AutoMix needs a following track to prepare a transition.

What happens if I skip a song?

Skipping or seeking cancels the transition being prepared. Playback continues normally, and AutoMix can prepare again for a later song.

Where can I see what happened?

Open Mod Settings → AutoMix and check Now and Last transition for the current status and latest result.
